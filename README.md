**📊 Content Analytics & Trend Exploration Dashboard**


This interactive dashboard provides a comprehensive view of media content trends across genres, languages, and release years. It is designed to help analysts, researchers, and content strategists explore how media evolves over time and identify key insights.


**🔑 Key Features**

-** Summary Metrics:**
- Total Titles: 387
- Average Duration: 127.39 minutes
- Total Genres: 18
- Total Languages: 4
- Genre & Language Distribution:
Visual breakdown of content across genres (Action, Comedy, Drama, etc.) with language segmentation (English, Hindi, Tamil, Gujarati).
- Release Trends Over Time:
Line charts showing content growth from 2010–2025, highlighting peaks in production years.
- Language Share Analysis:


**Donut chart illustrating percentage share of titles by language:**
- Hindi: 41.86%
- English: 24.81%
- Tamil: 19.9%
- Gujarati: 13.44%
- Content Duration Insights:
Distribution of average content duration across bins (0–50, 50–100, 100–150, 150–200 minutes).
Benchmark gauge showing average duration relative to maximum observed.

**🎯 Purpose of this dashboard enables:****
- Trend exploration across genres and languages
- Comparative analysis of content growth by year
- Duration benchmarking for production planning
- Strategic insights for media houses, streaming platforms, and researchers
